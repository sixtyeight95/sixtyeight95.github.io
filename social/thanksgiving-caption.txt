"Thanksgiving dinner for 10 costs $55." Wait, what? 🦃

That's the American Farm Bureau's famous yearly survey, and it's a BARE-BONES menu: a 16-lb turkey, one box of stuffing, sweet potatoes, rolls, peas, cranberries, a veggie tray, two pumpkin pies, milk and coffee. No ham, no mashed potatoes, no drinks. And it's a national average; the same dinner was about $83 to $85 in the Northeast and West.

So it's not your real grocery bill. But because they price the SAME menu every year since 1986, it shows a real trend:
📅 1986: 3.2 hours of work to buy it
📈 2022: 2.3 hours (the inflation spike)
🦃 2025: 1.7 hours, the least ever

What does YOUR Thanksgiving actually cost to host? 👇
