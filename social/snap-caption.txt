You've probably seen headlines about cutting SNAP food aid. Here are the actual facts, straight from USDA's own data. 🛒

💵 Average benefit: about $189 a month per person, or about $2.07 per meal
👨‍👩‍👧 79% of SNAP households include a child, a senior, or someone with a disability
📉 73% of households are at or below the poverty line
👥 About 36 million people get SNAP

The proposed rule (Nov. 10): USDA estimates 6.3 million people would lose SNAP by 2029. It mainly affects families a bit above SNAP's normal income or savings limits who qualify through their state's rules.

USDA says it keeps aid within the program's normal limits and saves about $45 billion. Critics say it hits working families and 1.78 million kids' automatic free school meals.

It's not final. Public comments are open until December 10 at regulations.gov.

Did any of these numbers surprise you? 👇
