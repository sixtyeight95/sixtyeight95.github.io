Applying for jobs and hearing nothing back? It's not just you. 💼

The government's numbers show a "no hire, no fire" job market:
✅ Layoffs: 1.0% of jobs a month, near a record low
🐢 Hiring: 3.4% a month, down from 3.9% in 2019
⏳ The average job seeker has been looking 27 weeks, up from 22 in 2019
📉 28% of job seekers have been out of work 6+ months

So if you have a job, it's relatively safe. If you lose one, plan for a longer search than a few years ago.
