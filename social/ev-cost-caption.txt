Does an electric car actually save you money? We did the math, per year. 🚗⚡

Total cost to own a new 2026 vehicle, 15,000 miles a year (AAA):
🚙 Medium sedan: EV $13.7k | Gas $10.6k | Hybrid $10.2k
🚐 Compact SUV: EV $11.9k | Gas $11.7k | Hybrid $11.2k
🚚 Pickup: EV $17.4k | Gas $16.6k | Hybrid $16.1k

EVs DO save on running costs: about $1,700 a year less on fuel and maintenance for a sedan.

But they lose value fast. Depreciation runs about $6,800 a year for a new electric sedan vs $3,300 for gas. And the $7,500 federal tax credit ended in September 2025.

The surprise winner in every class: hybrids.

EVs make the most sense if you charge at home, drive a lot, keep your car for years, or buy used.

EV owners, does your experience match? 👇
