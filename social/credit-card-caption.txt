Putting the holidays on a credit card? Here's what $1,000 of gifts really costs at today's 22.4% average card rate. 💳

🔴 Minimum payments only: about $1,788 total, and nearly 6 years to pay off
🟡 $50 a month: $263 in interest, done in about 2 years
🟢 $100 a month: $117 in interest, done in 12 months
🟢 $200 a month: $60 in interest, done in 6 months

Card rates have gone from about 17% in 2019 to 22.4% now, and the Fed has started raising rates again.

Best move: pay it in full if you can. If not, pick a number bigger than the minimum and stick to it.
