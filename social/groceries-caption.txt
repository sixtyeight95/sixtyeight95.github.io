POST CAPTION
------------
Groceries feel insanely expensive. But are they? 🛒

We stopped asking "how much does it cost" and asked "how long do you have to WORK to buy it?"

Since 2019, prices jumped. But paychecks are up 37% too. So in minutes of work:

🥛 Milk, bread, chicken: about the same as 2019
☕ Coffee: +67% more work
🥩 Ground beef: +30%
🥚 Eggs: +25%

And the wild part: compared with 1985, 12 of 13 groceries take LESS work to buy today. The only exception is beef.

What's the one grocery item that makes you wince at checkout? 👇
