"EV batteries are so dirty to make that electric cars aren't really greener." True or false? 🔋🌎

We ran the numbers with EPA, EIA and research data:
🏭 Building an EV battery creates about 4.5 extra tons of CO2 before it drives a mile
🛣️ But the EV pays that back after about 24,000 miles, under 2 years of normal driving
📉 Over its life, a U.S. EV produces roughly half to two-thirds less CO2 than a gas car

So on carbon: false. EVs win, clearly.

But "zero emissions" hides something real. Lithium mining drains scarce desert water, some cobalt in Congo is mined with child labor, and recycling is still catching up. EVs shrink the damage. They also move it somewhere else.

Did this change your mind either way? 👇
