"Insulin costs $5 to make." Is that true? 💉

We dug into the research. Yes: one vial costs about $3 to $9 to make, profit included.

What wealthy countries paid per vial in 2022:
🇬🇧 UK: $22
🇫🇷 France: $22
🇩🇪 Germany: $31
🇯🇵 Japan: $32
🇨🇦 Canada: $38
🇺🇸 U.S.: $227

But here's the twist: $227 was the sticker price. After secret drugmaker discounts, U.S. insurers paid about $54. Still 2.3x other countries, and the uninsured often paid full sticker.

Since 2023, list prices were cut 65 to 78%, Medicare caps insulin at $35 a month, and California's new insulin costs about $11 a pen.
