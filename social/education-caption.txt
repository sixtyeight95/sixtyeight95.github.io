College got WAY more expensive than everything else. But something changed in 2016 that almost nobody noticed. 🎓

Since 1980:
📈 College tuition: 13.8x
💵 Hourly pay: 4.7x
🛒 Everything else: 4.0x

So a year of college takes nearly 3x as many hours of work as it did in 1980.

The twist: it peaked in 2016. Since then, paychecks have grown faster than tuition, and a year of college takes about 19% FEWER hours of work than at the peak.

Still way worse than 1980. But not "only ever getting worse" anymore.

Did you (or your kids) go to college, skip it, or pay it off already? 👇
