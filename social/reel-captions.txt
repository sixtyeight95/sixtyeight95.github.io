4 - GAS PRICES REEL
-------------------
Does the president set gas prices? 33 years of data say: not directly. ⛽

Crude oil explains 92% of the swings. Presidents can nudge oil with wars, sanctions and the emergency reserve, but the cheapest AND priciest presidencies were from the same party.
