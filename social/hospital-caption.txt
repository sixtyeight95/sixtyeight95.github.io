Why are hospital bills so insanely expensive? 🏥

The same hip replacement, what private insurers paid in 2022:
🇪🇸 Spain: $6,780
🇩🇪 Germany: $7,681
🇬🇧 UK: $14,849
🇺🇸 U.S.: $29,006

Where the money goes:
1️⃣ Private insurers pay hospitals 254% of what Medicare pays
2️⃣ In 47% of metro areas, just 1 or 2 health systems run every hospital
3️⃣ About 25% of hospital budgets go to administration, about twice Canada's share
4️⃣ "List prices" average $4.32 for every $1 of actual cost, and the uninsured are the ones billed them

Since 1980, hospital prices are up 17x. Paychecks: 4.7x.
